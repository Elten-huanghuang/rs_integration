**Choose a recipe. Preview the plan. Let your machines do the work.**

RS Integration (RSI) adds recursive crafting, remote machine access, and storage tools for **Refined Storage** and **Beyond Dimensions**.

[中文介绍](https://github.com/Elten-huanghuang/rs_integration/blob/master/README_zh.md) · [User guide](https://github.com/Elten-huanghuang/rs_integration/blob/master/docs/guide/README.md)

## What you can do

- **Craft across mods:** start from JEI/EMI, inspect the recipe tree and missing materials, choose alternatives, and run supported machines. Select inventory, the current storage network, and Sophisticated Backpacks as material sources.
- **Manage machines remotely:** bind machines, open compatible GUIs, and share repeated work across multiple machines. Press `P` for plans, progress, and machine management.
- **Expand your storage:** browse items and fluids in one mixed terminal, or store items, fluids, and magic resources in a Myriad Realms Guixu Disk for RS.
- **Use stored items:** Resonance Disks provide supported passive effects and resource access; resonant containers connect to native Botania and Ars Nouveau systems.
- **Reduce routine work:** transfer container contents with `F`, order food, replenish trades and reforging materials, and use FTB Quests and Sophisticated Backpack integrations.
- **Make it yours:** search plans and machines, use pinyin-aware search, switch UI themes, and edit settings in game.

## Requirements

**Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge 47+ · JEI on the client.** Install the same RSI version on the client and server. EMI support also requires JEI.

Refined Storage 1.12+ and Beyond Dimensions are optional. Ordinary crafting-table recipes work recursively from inventory alone. RS and BD remain separate networks; their balances are never combined automatically.

## Quick start

1. Connect an RS or BD network, or begin with inventory-only crafting.
2. For machine recipes, hold a compatible network linker or terminal and use `Alt` + right-click to bind a machine, or press `;` nearby to scan.
3. Open a recipe in JEI/EMI and click the RSI craft action.
4. Review the plan, choose material sources, recipes, quantity, and output destination, then confirm.
5. Press `P` to follow progress. Cancel in the screen or with `/rsi cancel`.

## Integrations and help

Supported integrations include Botania, Ars Nouveau, Goety, Malum, Embers/Aetherworks, Farmer's Delight and addons, Apotheosis, Iron's Spells 'n Spellbooks, and more. Each module loads only with its target mod. Support is recipe-specific; native machine requirements and some manual ritual steps still apply.
